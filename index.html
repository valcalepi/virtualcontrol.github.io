About

VMware Cloud Foundation architect with 25 years of hands-on enterprise virtualization experience, spanning design, deployment, validation and root-cause troubleshooting across the full VCF 9 stack.

My most recent engagement delivered a VCF Operations 9 monitoring design for a Fortune 100 energy utility through a Broadcom Premier Partner, issued as a client-facing High-Level Design with production build guides, per-team hand-off packets, and a stakeholder Q&A brief. Before that I was sole architect and sole engineer for a complete VMware estate across a nine-year engagement, including full ownership of the storage estate.

I document design decisions as decision, justification and implication — each traced to the requirement it serves and cited to Broadcom source. That discipline runs through everything here: a 60-page architecture design playbook where every claim carries a source and a verification date, an engagement handbook a delivery team can execute consistently, and an HLD generator that blocks its own output when an intake violates a published VCF limit.

As founder of Virtual Control LLC, I run a full nested VCF 9.0.1 environment end-to-end — vSphere, vSAN, NSX, SDDC Manager, VCF Operations and Aria Automation — for capability development, testing and client enablement. The library below is the output of that practice: real recovery work, not theory.
